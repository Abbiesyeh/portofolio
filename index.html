ABOUT

Who is Abbie

I'm a computer science graduate from Universitas Lampung who moved into healthcare analytics. The work sits at an unusual intersection: the questions come from clinical and operational reality — why a bed is still occupied, where the pharmacy queue stalls — and the answers have to hold up when an auditor asks about them.

Day to day I write and optimise SQL against hospital systems, shape the output into dashboards in Google Looker Studio, and sit with the units that use them until the numbers actually change a decision. I hold a second role in business control, so the same data also feeds compliance reviews, audit follow-up and management reporting.

My rule: a dashboard is an operational tool, not a report. If nobody opens it on a Tuesday morning, it doesn't count.

FAQ

Good to know

What do you actually build?

Dashboards that a unit opens daily, backed by SQL that I write and tune myself. The RS YMC system is 35 of them under one entry point, split into operations and service on one side and support and analytics on the other.

Which tools do you work in?

SQL as the base, Google Looker Studio for the interface, and Excel or Google Sheets where a quick model beats a dashboard. Most of my time goes to the unglamorous part: cleaning and shaping the data so the numbers on screen can be trusted.

How do you usually start?

With the decision, not the data. I ask which choice the dashboard is supposed to support and who makes it, then work backwards to the metric, the query and the refresh rate. Anything that doesn't serve that decision doesn't make it onto the screen.

Do you only work in healthcare?

That's where my experience is deepest — hospital operations, service quality, claims and internal control. The underlying work is the same anywhere operational data needs to become a decision, so I'm open to other sectors.

What's your background?

Bachelor of Computer Science from Universitas Lampung, GPA 3.67, with data analytics bootcamps at RevoU and KarirNex. My thesis applied CNNs to image classification of traditional Lampung batik patterns.

Are you available?

Yes, for data analyst roles. I'm based in Lampung Tengah, Indonesia, and open to on-site, hybrid or remote. Email is the fastest way to reach me.
